# Possible future LLM tax-forecasting experiment

Status: unstarted design idea, not evidence. No paid API experiment is authorized
by the September 7 capacity sprint.

Rees-Jones and Taubinsky (2020) Study 1 elicited total federal income-tax forecasts
for a simplified hypothetical taxpayer, then studied local and nonlocal slopes.
It did not directly establish this project's illustrative 0.12 latent error RMSE.
See the [primary article](https://doi.org/10.1093/restud/rdz045), sections 3.1-3.3.

A future experiment could compare human and model forecasts on the same
instrument, with pinned model versions, prompts, tax-schedule provenance and a
preregistered analysis. Report signed bias, dispersion, MAE and RMSE separately.
Model responses would describe the tested models and prompts, not identify human
behavior or population welfare costs.

Comprehensive tax errors cannot be inferred to exceed federal-only errors:
component biases can cancel, and covariance affects dispersion. Neither a
positive result nor any lower-bound relationship should be presumed.
